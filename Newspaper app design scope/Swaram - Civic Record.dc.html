THE CIVIC RECORD
MOBILE EDITION·NINE SCREENS·15 AUGUST 2026

A modern civic platform presented through the visual language of an old newspaper. Every problem becomes a record; every record creates pressure; every response becomes evidence.

ACT I SEE & REPORT A citizen arrives, reads the street, files a record.
ACT II TRACK & PRESSURE The record accumulates weight, geography and a queue of the waiting.
ACT III RESPOND, VERIFY & ACCOUNT Evidence is filed, the community rules on it, the week is printed.
